【参考回答-英文】
If a project is required to purchase and lock tokens, that portion should not be understood as the Grant itself. It is part of the project’s ecosystem participation, long-term alignment, or cooperation conditions.
A Grant refers to support provided by BOT Chain to ecosystem projects, including resources, incentives, technical support, marketing support, or ecosystem coordination. Token purchase and lockup arrangements belong to the project’s own participation arrangement and should be distinguished from Grant support.
Whether purchase, lockup, release schedule, incentive conditions, or project obligations are included will be subject to the formal cooperation documents confirmed by both parties.

### Bridge security concerns (ThorChain / Monad) / Bridge 安全担忧

【相关问题】如何回应类似 ThorChain、Monad 等近期 Bridge 安全事件带来的担忧？ / How should BOT Chain respond to concerns caused by recent Bridge security incidents such as ThorChain and Monad？
【关键词】Bridge安全;ThorChain;Monad;跨链安全;审计
【参考回答-中文】
Bridge 安全是整个多链行业长期关注的重点。BOT Chain 对跨链安全采取审慎态度，不会以“绝对安全”作为承诺，而是通过公开审计、持续监控、风险隔离和分阶段上线机制降低风险。
BOT Chain Bridge 当前采用 Lock / Release 机制，并已提供公开审计材料。用户和合作项目可参考 BOT Bridge 审计报告、CertiK 页面及官方文档了解安全设计。后续如涉及更高额度资产、更多链或更多跨链场景，BOT Chain 将根据实际使用规模推进安全复审、监控机制和必要的风控限制。
跨链桥属于高风险基础设施，安全建设需要持续进行。BOT Chain 会优先保障资产安全和透明披露，在功能扩展前保持审慎评估，并避免对任何跨链系统作出绝对无风险的承诺。
审计材料：https://bridge.botchain.ai/docs/Bridge-Audit-Report.pdf ；CertiK：https://skynet.certik.com/projects/botchain
【参考回答-英文】
Bridge security is a long-term priority across the multi-chain industry. BOT Chain takes a cautious approach to cross-chain security and does not make “absolute safety” claims. Instead, it reduces risk through public audits, ongoing monitoring, risk isolation, and phased rollout mechanisms.
BOT Chain Bridge currently uses a Lock / Release mechanism and has provided public audit materials. Users and ecosystem partners can refer to the BOT Bridge audit report, the CertiK page, and official documentation to understand the security design. If higher asset volumes, more chains, or additional cross-chain scenarios are involved in the future, BOT Chain will continue to advance security re-reviews, monitoring mechanisms, and necessary risk-control limits based on actual usage scale.
Cross-chain bridges are high-risk infrastructure, and security must be treated as an ongoing process. BOT Chain will prioritize asset security and transparent disclosure, maintain cautious evaluation before expanding functionality, and avoid making absolute no-risk commitments for any cross-chain system.
Audit: https://bridge.botchain.ai/docs/Bridge-Audit-Report.pdf ; CertiK: https://skynet.certik.com/projects/botchain

### Grant tiers / Grant 档位

【相关问题】Grant 档位如何计算？是否参考市场价格？ / How are Grant tiers calculated? Are they based on market prices？
【关键词】Grant档位;tier;TVL;市场价格
【参考回答-中文】
Grant 档位会综合评估项目类型、开发工作量、生态贡献、上线阶段、预期用户规模、链上交互深度以及对 BOT Chain 生态的长期价值。
在制定档位时，BOT Chain 会参考同类型 Layer 1 生态扶持计划和市场通行标准，但不会只按单一市场价格或固定模板计算。不同类型项目的评估重点也会不同，例如 DeFi 更关注 TVL、交易量和流动性贡献；GameFi 更关注真实用户、交互频次和留存；Infra/RWA 项目则更关注技术集成难度、长期使用价值和生态必要性。
最终 Grant 金额和支持方式会根据项目具体情况进行评估，并以双方确认的合作条款为准。
【参考回答-英文】
Grant tiers are evaluated based on project type, development workload, ecosystem contribution, launch stage, expected user scale, depth of on-chain interaction, and long-term value to the BOT Chain ecosystem.
When setting Grant tiers, BOT Chain references similar Layer 1 ecosystem support programs and common market standards, but does not calculate support based only on a single market price or fixed template. Different project categories have different evaluation priorities. For example, DeFi projects are assessed more on TVL, transaction volume, and liquidity contribution; GameFi projects are assessed more on real users, interaction frequency, and retention; Infra and RWA projects are assessed more on technical integration difficulty, long-term usage value, and ecosystem necessity.
The final Grant amount and support format will be evaluated based on the specific project and confirmed in cooperation terms agreed by both parties.
