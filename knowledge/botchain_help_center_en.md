Getting Started Add BOT Chain Mainnet to MetaMask

BOT Chain Mainnet Parameters

Field Mainnet Value Note

Network Name BOT Chain Mainnet Network display name.

RPC URL https://rpc.botchain.ai Official mainnet RPC.

Chain ID 677 Must be 677.

Currency Symbol BOT Used for Gas.

Block Explorer https://scan.botchain.ai Address and transaction lookup.

STEP 1 | Open Network Management

Open the MetaMask extension, select the menu in the upper-right corner, and choose Networks.

Official MetaMask Help Center interface: select Networks from the upper-right menu.

STEP 2 | Choose Add a Custom Network

On Manage networks, scroll to Add a custom network. If BOT Chain is not listed by default, do not select a similarly named network.

Select Add a custom network at the bottom of the page.

STEP 3 | Enter Mainnet Values

Enter these values exactly: Network name: BOT Chain Mainnet; Default RPC URL: https://rpc.botchain.ai ; Chain ID: 677; Currency symbol: BOT; Block explorer URL: https://scan.botchain.ai . Never use an RPC supplied in a private message.

BOT Chain completed-form example based on the official MetaMask custom network form. Verify every field, especially the RPC URL and Chain ID 677, before saving.

STEP 4 | Save and Switch Networks

Confirm that the Chain ID is 677 and select Save. Return to the wallet and switch to BOT Chain Mainnet from the network selector.

STEP 5 | Verify the Address and Gas

Copy the active wallet address and verify it on scan.botchain.ai. A small BOT balance is required for Gas before sending or swapping.

Recheck the Network After Changing Devices: After changing devices, reinstalling, or switching MetaMask products, verify that BOT Chain Mainnet exists and that its values are correct. Add it again only if it is missing.

Getting Started Get BOT for BOT Chain Gas

STEP 1 | Confirm the Wallet Address and Network

Switch to BOT Chain Mainnet and confirm that the active address is the address that needs Gas. Testnet assets cannot pay Mainnet Gas.

STEP 2 | Check the BOT Balance

Check the address in the wallet or at https://scan.botchain.ai . A wallet may hold tokens but still be unable to transact when it has no BOT.

STEP 3 | Open the Official BOT Bridge

Visit https://bridge.botchain.ai , connect the correct wallet address, and choose BOT Chain as the destination.

BOT Bridge page example. Supported source networks, assets, BOT amounts, and fees depend on the current interface.

STEP 4 | Review the BOT Gas Option

If the page offers an option to receive BOT for future Gas, review the live BOT amount and cost before selecting it. Do not rely on the option when the page shows zero, a blank value, or incomplete information.

STEP 5 | Verify Arrival

After completion, verify in the wallet or explorer that BOT reached the active address. Do not assume BOT is included unless the page states it clearly before submission.

STEP 6 | Retry the Original Action

Retry only after BOT arrives. If the transaction still fails, check the network, estimated Gas, transaction details, and error message. Do not submit repeatedly.

Other Ways to Obtain BOT: Use only currently supported official channels that you can verify independently. Do not accept private Gas top-up offers, BOT sales, or requests to transfer funds first.

Wallet & Accounts Create a BO Wallet

STEP 1 | Download the Official App

Left: open BO Wallet and choose Create Wallet. Right: set and confirm the 6-digit PIN.

Network & Transactions Use BOT Chain Explorer to Check Addresses and Transactions

STEP 1 | Open the Official Explorer

Visit https://scan.botchain.ai , verify the address bar, and locate the search box on the home page.

The BOT Chain Explorer search box accepts addresses, transaction hashes, blocks, and tokens.

STEP 2 | Search a Wallet Address

Paste the full 0x wallet address and search. A normal address page shows the BOT balance, transactions, and token transfers. Available tabs depend on the address type.

STEP 3 | Search a TxHash

Copy the full 0x TxHash from the wallet, Bridge, or B DEX and paste it into the search box. The transaction page shows Status, From, To, Value, Gas, and time.

Actual transaction details example: Success means the transaction executed. Also verify the full TxHash, From, To, Value, Transaction fee, and Timestamp.

STEP 4 | Understand Transaction Status

Success means the transaction executed, Pending means it is waiting, and Failed means execution failed. A failed transaction usually does not transfer the asset, but used Gas may still be charged.
